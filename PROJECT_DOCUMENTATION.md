# Average of the selective results for all the subjects over the years(2015-2025)
> Problem being addressed: Students are not choosing the right schools for their selective exams, so this program would help them see how students in the past have performed for this exam.  
> Purpose: "Students that are taking the selective exam are not choosing the correct options due to lack of knowledge about what to choose."
> Actor: Student(Primary)
> Goal: To predict the marks for different subjects that a student who taking the selective exam would get around. Based on this prediction the students that use this system interface can see what they are capable and take further decisions throughout the period leading up to and after they take the selective exam.
> Preconditions:
- The student has to be logged into the system interface 
- The student has to be verified as a student with proper login credentials to access the system interface
- The program has to be showing selective schools only not the other ones
> Main Flow:
1. A student opens the system interface
2. They log in with their student credentials
3. Their login is successful
4. The program gives the averages of the selective exam results from 2015 to 2025
5. The student knows what school options to choose for their exam because they have a general idea of what they might get
6. The program is successful and has acheived what is was made to
